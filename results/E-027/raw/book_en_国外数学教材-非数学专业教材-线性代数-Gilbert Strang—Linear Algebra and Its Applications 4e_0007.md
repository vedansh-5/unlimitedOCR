<|det|>text [138, 735, 910, 812]<|/det|>The two fundamental problems are \( Ax = b \) and \( Ax = \lambda x \) for square matrices \( A \). The first problem \( Ax = b \) has a solution when \( A \) has independent columns. The second problem \( Ax = \lambda x \) looks for independent eigenvectors. A crucial part of this course is to learn what "independence" means.
