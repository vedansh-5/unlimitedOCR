<|det|>text [75, 400, 861, 498]<|/det|>Designed to improve access to federally conducted and federally assisted programs/activities for LEP persons
<|det|>text [75, 508, 888, 604]<|/det|>Federal agencies were asked to develop and implement a system to provide LEP persons access to services.
