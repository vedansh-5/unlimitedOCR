\( \because {AD}//{BC}\therefore {\Delta MAD} \sim  {\Delta MBC} \)

\( \because \frac{MA}{MB} = \frac{4}{5} \therefore \frac{MA}{MA + 4} = \frac{4}{5} \therefore MA = 16 \)

\( \frac{MD}{MC} = \frac{4}{5} \therefore \frac{MD}{MD + 5} = \frac{4}{5} \therefore MD = 20 \)

\( \therefore {\Delta MAD} \) 周长 \( = {MA} + {AD} + {MD} = {16} + 8 + {20} \)

\( \therefore \) 由①②可知。\( {\Delta MBC} \) 周长 = 55
