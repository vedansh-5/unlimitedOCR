3.3. Comparison with other electrodes in electrochemical performance

As noble metal Pt and  \( RuO_{2} \)  have been regarded as the state-of-the-art HER and OER catalysts, commercial Pt/C,  \( RuO_{2} \)  deposited on the GC electrodes were used to electrolysis splitting water at the same condition with the  \( CW@Cu^{I/II}NPs \)  (Fig. 5). As shown in Fig. 5a, the potential of the proposed electrode for splitting water is more positive than Pt/C about 0.35 V with 10 mA cm \( ^{-2} \)  of current density, although the

proposed electrode is worked for OER and Pt/C working for HER. From Fig. 5b can be seen that the potential of the proposed electrode for ORE with \(400\mathrm{mAcm}^{-2}\) of current density is less negative than \(\mathrm{RuO_2}\), although its onset potential is larger than \(\mathrm{RuO_2}\).
