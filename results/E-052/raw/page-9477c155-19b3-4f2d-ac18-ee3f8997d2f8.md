<|det|>title [142, 117, 776, 132]<|/det|>G.1.3. BOUNDS ON THE SECOND MOMENT ALONG THE FINITE-PARTICLE SYSTEMS
<|det|>text [141, 140, 854, 174]<|/det|>In this section, we show that along the finite-particle dynamics (16) and algorithm (20), the distributions remain in  \( \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{R}^{2dN}) \) .
<|det|>text [141, 175, 865, 226]<|/det|>Under Assumption 1, recall from Theorem 35 (in Section H) that there exists a unique equilibrium point \( z^{*} = (x^{*},y^{*})\in \mathbb{R}^{2d} \) which satisfies \( \nabla V(z^{*}) = 0 \). Define \( \mathbf{z}^{*} = (x^{*},\dots ,x^{*},y^{*},\dots ,y^{*})\in \mathbb{R}^{2dN} \). Then by construction, \( b^{\mathbf{Z}}(\mathbf{z}^{*}) = 0 \), where \( b^{\mathbf{Z}} \) is the vector field defined in (17).
<|det|>text [141, 240, 855, 276]<|/det|>Lemma 31 Assume Assumption 1. Suppose \(\mathbf{Z}_t\sim \rho_t^{\mathbf{Z}}\) evolves following the finite-particle dynamics (16) in \(\mathbb{R}^{2dN}\) from \(\mathbf{Z}_0\sim \rho_0^{\mathbf{Z}}\in \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{R}^{2dN})\). Then \(\rho_t^{\mathbf{Z}}\in \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{R}^{2dN})\) for all \(t\geq 0\).
<|det|>text [141, 288, 855, 359]<|/det|>Proof We note that  \( \rho_{t}^{Z} \)  is absolutely continuous with respect to the Lebesgue measure on  \( R^{2dN} \)  by virtue of the Brownian motion component in the dynamics (16). We now show that the second moment of  \( \rho_{t}^{Z} \)  remains finite for all  \( t \geq 0 \) . Since  \( Z_{t} \)  evolves following the dynamics (16), its density  \( \rho_{t}^{Z} \)  evolves following the Fokker-Planck equation:
<|det|>equation [381, 370, 617, 405]<|/det|>\[
\frac {\partial \rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}}}{\partial t} = - \nabla \cdot \left(\rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}} b ^ {\mathbf {Z}}\right) + \tau \Delta \rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}}.
\]
<|det|>text [142, 415, 538, 432]<|/det|>From this, we can compute, using integration by parts:
<|det|>equation [140, 440, 881, 697]<|/det|>\[
\begin{array}{l} \frac {d}{d t} \mathbb {E} _ {\rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}}} \left[ \| \mathbf {Z} _ {t} - \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \| ^ {2} \right] = \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {2 d N}} \frac {\partial \rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}} (\mathbf {z})}{\partial t} \| \mathbf {z} - \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \| ^ {2} d \mathbf {z} \\ = \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {2 d N}} \left(- \nabla \cdot \left(\rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}} b ^ {\mathbf {Z}}\right) (\mathbf {z}) + \tau \Delta \rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}} (\mathbf {z})\right) \| \mathbf {z} - \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \| ^ {2} d \mathbf {z} \\ = \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {2 d N}} \rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}} (\mathbf {z}) \left\langle b ^ {\mathbf {Z}} (\mathbf {z}), \nabla \left(\| \mathbf {z} - \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \| ^ {2}\right) \right\rangle d \mathbf {z} + \tau \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {2 d N}} \rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}} (\mathbf {z}) \Delta \left(\| \mathbf {z} - \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \| ^ {2}\right) d \mathbf {z} \\ = 2 \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {2 d N}} \rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}} (\mathbf {z}) \left\langle b ^ {\mathbf {Z}} (\mathbf {z}), \mathbf {z} - \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \right\rangle d \mathbf {z} + \tau \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {2 d N}} \rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}} (\mathbf {z}) (4 d N) d \mathbf {z} \\ = 2 \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {2 d N}} \rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}} (\mathbf {z}) \left\langle b ^ {\mathbf {Z}} (\mathbf {z}) - b ^ {\mathbf {Z}} (\mathbf {z} ^ {*}), \mathbf {z} - \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \right\rangle d \mathbf {z} + 4 \tau d N \\ \leq - 2 \alpha \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {2 d N}} \rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}} (\mathbf {z}) \| \mathbf {z} - \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \| ^ {2} d \mathbf {z} + 4 \tau d N \\ = - 2 \alpha \mathbb {E} _ {\rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}}} \left[ \| \mathbf {Z} _ {t} - \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \| ^ {2} \right] + 4 \tau d N \\ \end{array}
\]
<|det|>text [142, 705, 854, 738]<|/det|>where the inequality above uses the property that  \( -b^{Z} \)  is  \( \alpha \) -strongly monotone, from Lemma 12. We can write the differential inequality above equivalently as:
<|det|>equation [335, 750, 658, 784]<|/det|>\[
\frac {d}{d t} \left(e ^ {2 \alpha t} \mathbb {E} _ {\rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}}} \left[ \| \mathbf {Z} _ {t} - \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \| ^ {2} \right]\right) \leq e ^ {2 \alpha t} 4 \tau d N.
\]
<|det|>text [142, 794, 542, 810]<|/det|>Integrating from 0 to t and rearranging the result gives:
<|det|>equation [249, 820, 748, 892]<|/det|>\[
\begin{array}{l} \mathbb {E} _ {\rho_ {t} ^ {\mathbf {Z}}} \left[ \| \mathbf {Z} _ {t} - \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \| ^ {2} \right] \leq e ^ {- 2 \alpha t} \mathbb {E} _ {\rho_ {0} ^ {\mathbf {Z}}} \left[ \| \mathbf {Z} _ {0} - \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \| ^ {2} \right] + \frac {(1 - e ^ {- 2 \alpha t})}{\alpha} 2 \tau d N \\ \leq \mathbb {E} _ {\rho_ {0} ^ {\mathbf {Z}}} \left[ \| \mathbf {Z} _ {0} - \left. \mathbf {z} ^ {*} \right\| ^ {2} \right] + \frac {2 \tau d N}{\alpha}. \\ \end{array}
\]
