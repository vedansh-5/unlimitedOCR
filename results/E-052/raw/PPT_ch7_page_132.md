<|det|>header [493, 20, 959, 99]<|/det|>7.9 Vector Spaces, Inner Product Spaces, Linear Transformations Optional
<|det|>text [54, 130, 923, 238]<|/det|>Vectors whose inner product is zero are called orthogonal. The length or norm of a vector in V is defined by
<|det|>equation [55, 255, 478, 323]<|/det|>\[
\| \mathbf {a} \| = \sqrt {(\mathbf {a} , \mathbf {a})} \quad (\geq 0). \tag {2}
\]
<|det|>text [54, 325, 877, 432]<|/det|>A vector of norm 1 is called a unit vector. From these axioms and from (2) one can derive the basic inequality
<|det|>equation [55, 445, 906, 509]<|/det|>\[
\| \mathbf {a}, \mathbf {b} \| \leq \| \mathbf {a} \| \| \mathbf {b} \| \quad (C a u c h y - S c h w a r z i n e q u a l i t y) \tag {3}
\]
<|det|>text [55, 517, 322, 560]<|/det|>From this follows
<|det|>equation [55, 583, 918, 645]<|/det|>\[
\| \mathbf {a} + \mathbf {b} \| \leq \| \mathbf {a} \| + \| \mathbf {b} \| \quad (T r i a n g l e i n e q u a l i t y). \tag {4}
\]
<|det|>text [55, 655, 541, 707]<|/det|>A simple direct calculation gives
<|det|>equation [55, 707, 918, 775]<|/det|>\[
\| \mathbf {a} + \mathbf {b} \| ^ {2} + \| \mathbf {a} - \mathbf {b} \| ^ {2} = 2 (\| \mathbf {a} \| ^ {2} + \| \mathbf {b} \| ^ {2}) \quad (P a r a l l e l o g r a m e q u a l i t y). \tag {5}
\]
