<|det|>title [47, 82, 502, 156]<|/det|>The Quotient Rule
<|det|>text [53, 221, 881, 380]<|/det|>We find a rule for differentiating the quotient of two differentiable functions  \( u = f(x) \)  and  \( v = g(x) \)  in much the same way that we found the Product Rule.
<|det|>text [53, 448, 906, 553]<|/det|>If x, u, and v change by amounts  \( \Delta x \) ,  \( \Delta u \) , and  \( \Delta v \) , then the corresponding change in the quotient u/v is
<|det|>equation [166, 616, 823, 885]<|/det|>\[
\begin{array}{l} \Delta \left(\frac {u}{v}\right) = \frac {u + \Delta u}{v + \Delta v} - \frac {u}{v} = \frac {(u + \Delta u) v - u (v + \Delta v)}{v (v + \Delta v)} \\ = \frac {v \Delta u - u \Delta v}{v (v + \Delta v)} \\ \end{array}
\]
