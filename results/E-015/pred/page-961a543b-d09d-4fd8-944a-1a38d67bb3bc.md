3. 积分定理

\[
F (s) = L [ f (t) ] \Rightarrow L [ \int_ {0} ^ {t} f (t) d t ] = \frac {F (s)}{s} + \frac {1}{s} f ^ {(- 1)} (0)
\]

其中 \(f^{(-1)}(0) = \int f(t)dt\Big|_{t = 0}\)

\[
L [ \underbrace {\int_ {0} ^ {t} \cdots \int_ {0} ^ {t} f (t) d t \dots d t} _ {n} ] = \frac {F (s)}{s ^ {n}} + \frac {1}{s ^ {n}} f ^ {(- 1)} (0) + \dots + \frac {1}{s} f ^ {(- n)} (0)
\]

其中  \( f^{(-n)}(0)=\underbrace{\int_{0}^{t}\cdots\int_{0}^{t}}_{n}f(t)dt\ldots dt\bigg|_{t=0} \)
