Determinant of a Matrix

Used for inversion

If \(\operatorname{det}(A) = 0\), then \(A\) has no inverse

\[
A = \left[ \begin{array}{c c} a & b \\ c & d \end{array} \right] \quad \det (A) = a d - b c
\]

\[
A ^ {- 1} = \frac {1}{a d - b c} \left[ \begin{array}{c c} d & - b \\ - c & a \end{array} \right]
\]
