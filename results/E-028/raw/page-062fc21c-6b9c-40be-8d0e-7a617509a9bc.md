<|det|>title [378, 91, 616, 107]<|/det|>1.1.9【因式分解双换元类型】
<|det|>title [148, 119, 250, 134]<|/det|>▲适用题型：
<|det|>text [146, 147, 816, 162]<|/det|>双变量条件等式能因式分解的时候可以考虑双换元，整理成更容易利用不等式的结构；
<|det|>title [148, 175, 250, 189]<|/det|>▲方法原理：
<|det|>equation [147, 198, 589, 412]<|/det|>\[
\text {比如：} \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a ^ {2} - b ^ {2} = 1 \Rightarrow (a + b) (a - b) = 1 \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a + b = m \\ a - b = \frac {1}{m} \end{array} \right. \\ \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = \frac {1}{2} \binom {m + \frac {1}{m}} {m} \\ b = \frac {1}{2} \binom {m - \frac {1}{m}} {m} \end{array} \right. \\ \Rightarrow 2 a + b = \left(m + \frac {1}{m}\right) + \frac {1}{2} \left(m - \frac {1}{m}\right) = \frac {3 m}{2} + \frac {1}{2 m} \end{array} \right.
\]
<|det|>text [147, 424, 850, 439]<|/det|>关键在于观察条件等式能否因式分解，除了常见的初中所学的因式分解，还有一些可以配
<|det|>text [147, 449, 601, 489]<|/det|>凑分解，比如  \( \left\{\begin{aligned}ab+a+b=3&\Rightarrow ab+a+b=d(b+1)+b+1-1\\ =(a+1)(b+1)-1=3&\Rightarrow(a+1)(b+1)=4\end{aligned}\right. \)
