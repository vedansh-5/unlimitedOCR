<|det|>text [207, 130, 828, 175]<|/det|>Mixed Cations and Structural Complexity in \((\mathrm{Eu}_{1 - x}\mathrm{Ca}_x)_4\mathrm{In}_3\mathrm{Ge}_4\) and \((\mathrm{Eu}_{1 - x}\mathrm{Ca}_x)_3\mathrm{In}_2\mathrm{Ge}_3\) — The First Two Members of the Homologous Series
<|det|>text [207, 176, 900, 340]<|/det|>\(\mathrm{A}_{2[n + m]}\mathrm{In}_{2n + m}\mathrm{Ge}_{2[n + m]}\) (n, m = 1, 2, …∞; A: Ca, Sr, Ba, Eu, or Yb). — \(\mathrm{Eu}_{1.23}\mathrm{Ca}_{2.77}\mathrm{In}_3\mathrm{Ge}_4\) (I), \(\mathrm{Eu}_{2.60}\mathrm{Ca}_{1.40}\mathrm{In}_3\mathrm{Ge}_4\) (II), \(\mathrm{Eu}_{0.30}\mathrm{Ca}_{2.70}\mathrm{In}_2\mathrm{Ge}_3\) (III), and \(\mathrm{Eu}_{0.66}\mathrm{Ca}_{2.34}\mathrm{In}_2\mathrm{Ge}_3\) (IV) are synthesized from the elements in a molten In metal flux (960 °C, 20 h). The compounds are characterized by single crystal XRD, magnetic susceptibility measurements, and TB-LMTO band structure calculations. (I) and (II) crystallize in the space group C2/m with Z = 2, and (III) and (IV) in the space group Pnma with Z = 4. The polyanionic substructures of the compounds are based on \(\mathrm{InGe_4}\) edge-sharing tetrahedra, \(\mathrm{Ge_2}\) dimers, and bridging In atoms in a nearly square planar environment. — (YOU, T.-S.; TOBASH, P. H.; BOBEV*, S.; Inorg. Chem. 49 (2010) 4, 1773-1783; Dep. Chem. Biochem., Univ. Del., Newark, DE 19716, USA; Eng.) — W. Pewestorf
