<|det|>text [32, 206, 782, 276]<|/det|>■ Solution: Row operations on  \( [A\quad0] \)  produce
<|det|>equation [34, 292, 977, 691]<|/det|>\[
\left[ \begin{array}{r r r r} 3 & 5 & - 4 & 7 \\ - 3 & - 2 & 4 & - 1 \\ 6 & 1 & - 8 & - 4 \end{array} \right] \sim \left[ \begin{array}{r r r r} 1 & 0 & - \frac {4}{3} & - 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right], \quad x _ {1} - \frac {4}{3} x _ {3} = - 1 x _ {2} = 2 0 = 0
\]
<|det|>text [32, 778, 741, 900]<|/det|>Thus  \( x_{1} = -1 + \frac{4}{3}x_{3} \) ,  \( x_{2} = 2 \) , and  \( x_{3} \)  is free.
