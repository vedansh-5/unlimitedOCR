<|det|>text [165, 83, 869, 141]<|/det|>8. Let \( p \) be an odd prime. Prove that the Legendre symbol is a homomorphism from the multiplicative group \( (\mathbf{Z} / p\mathbf{Z})^{\times} \) into \( \{\pm 1\} \). What is the kernel of this homomorphism?
<|det|>text [165, 155, 710, 174]<|/det|>9. For every odd prime \( p \), define the Mersenne number
<|det|>equation [462, 189, 597, 211]<|/det|>\[
M _ {p} = 2 ^ {p} - 1.
\]
<|det|>text [194, 226, 865, 264]<|/det|>A prime number of the form \( M_p \) is called a Mersenne prime (see Exercise 5 in Section 1.5).
<|det|>text [194, 271, 506, 291]<|/det|>Let \( q \) be a prime divisor of \( M_p \).
<|det|>text [203, 303, 815, 345]<|/det|>(a) Prove that 2 has order \( p \) modulo \( q \), and so \( p \) divides \( q - 1 \). Hint: Fermat's theorem.
<|det|>text [203, 352, 618, 373]<|/det|>(b) Prove that \( p \) divides \( (q - 1) / 2 \), and so
<|det|>equation [468, 388, 637, 409]<|/det|>\[
q \equiv 1 \pmod {2 p}
\]
<|det|>text [243, 425, 286, 441]<|/det|>and
<|det|>equation [439, 441, 666, 464]<|/det|>\[
2 ^ {(q - 1) / 2} \equiv 1 \pmod {q}.
\]
<|det|>text [244, 474, 520, 493]<|/det|>Hint: Both \( p \) and \( q \) are odd.
<|det|>text [205, 499, 699, 528]<|/det|>(c) Prove that \(\left(\frac{2}{q}\right) = 1\), and so \(q \equiv \pm 1 \pmod{8}\).
<|det|>text [157, 540, 734, 560]<|/det|>10. For every positive integer \( n \), define the Fermat number
<|det|>equation [460, 574, 597, 595]<|/det|>\[
F _ {n} = 2 ^ {2 ^ {n}} + 1.
\]
<|det|>text [194, 612, 863, 650]<|/det|>A prime number of the form \( F_{n} \) is called a Fermat prime (see Exercise 7 in Section 1.5).
<|det|>text [194, 657, 641, 677]<|/det|>Let \( n \geq 2 \), and let \( q \) be a prime divisor of \( F_{n} \).
<|det|>text [205, 689, 625, 709]<|/det|>(a) Prove that 2 has order \(2^{n + 1}\) modulo \(q\).
<|det|>text [244, 713, 550, 730]<|/det|>Hint: Exercise 8 in Section 2.5.
<|det|>text [205, 739, 353, 758]<|/det|>(b) Prove that
<|det|>equation [452, 758, 651, 779]<|/det|>\[
q \equiv 1 \pmod {2 ^ {n + 1}}.
\]
<|det|>text [205, 792, 692, 812]<|/det|>(c) Prove that there exists an integer \(a\) such that
<|det|>equation [440, 826, 664, 852]<|/det|>\[
a ^ {2 ^ {n + 1}} \equiv - 1 \pmod {q}.
\]
<|det|>text [244, 866, 772, 897]<|/det|>Hint: Observe that \(\left(\frac{2}{q}\right) = 1\), and so \(2 \equiv a^2 \pmod{q}\).
<|det|>text [205, 902, 353, 921]<|/det|>(d) Prove that
<|det|>equation [452, 920, 651, 942]<|/det|>\[
q \equiv 1 \pmod {2 ^ {n + 2}}.
\]
