<|det|>title [54, 222, 258, 242]<|/det|>(二)n 母线系统
<|det|>text [62, 247, 792, 264]<|/det|>n 母线功率方程是这类方程的一般形式。由式并用结点注入功率代替结点电流后可得
<|det|>equation [333, 270, 664, 320]<|/det|>\[
\frac {P _ {i} - j Q _ {i}}{* _ {j}} = \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} Y _ {i j} U _ {j} \quad i = 1, 2, \dots , n
\]
<|det|>text [18, 325, 611, 342]<|/det|>式中 \(P_{i}, Q_{i}\) 分别为结点 \(i\) 的注入有功功率和注入无功功率，因而有
<|det|>equation [335, 349, 664, 381]<|/det|>\[
P _ {i} - j Q _ {i} = \sum_ {U _ {i} j = 1} ^ {*} Y _ {i j} U _ {j} \quad i = 1, 2, \dots , n
\]
<|det|>text [18, 386, 311, 406]<|/det|>使 \(\dot{U}_i = U_i\mathrm{e}^{\beta_i}\) ， \(Y_{ij} = G_{ij} + \mathrm{j}B_{ij}\) 则有
<|det|>equation [220, 411, 780, 455]<|/det|>\[
\begin{array}{l} P _ {i} - \mathrm{j} Q _ {i} = U _ {i} e ^ {- \mathrm{j} \delta_ {i}} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} (G _ {i j} + \mathrm{j} B _ {i j}) U _ {j} e ^ {\beta_ {j}} = U _ {i} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} (G _ {i j} + \mathrm{j} B _ {i j}) U _ {i} e ^ {- f \delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}}) \\ i = 1, 2, \dots , n \\ \end{array}
\]
<|det|>text [20, 460, 667, 479]<|/det|>由于 \(e^{-f\delta_i - \delta_j}) = \cos (\delta_i - \delta_j) - \mathrm{j}\sin (\delta_i - \delta_j)\)
<|det|>text [20, 481, 66, 497]<|/det|>故有
<|det|>equation [285, 506, 715, 574]<|/det|>\[
\begin{array}{l} P _ {i} - j Q _ {i} = U _ {i} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U _ {j} \left\{G _ {i j} \cos \left(\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}\right) + B _ {i j} \sin \left(\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}\right) \right. \\ + \int_ {i} B _ {i j} \cos (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) - G _ {i j} \sin (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) ] \} \\ i = 1, 2, \dots , n \\ \end{array}
\]
<|det|>text [19, 577, 291, 593]<|/det|>将上式的实部、虚部分开，则有
<|det|>equation [285, 602, 709, 682]<|/det|>\[
\left. \begin{array}{l} P _ {i} = U _ {i} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U _ {i} \left[ G _ {i j} \cos (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) + B _ {i j} \sin (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) \right] \\ Q _ {i} = - U _ {i} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U _ {i} \left[ B _ {i j} \cos (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) - G _ {i j} \sin (\delta_ {i} - \delta_ {j}) \right] \\ i = 1, 2, \dots , n \end{array} \right\}
\]
<|det|>text [19, 684, 114, 701]<|/det|>对节点 \(i\) ，
<|det|>equation [372, 707, 625, 724]<|/det|>\[
P _ {i} = P _ {G i} - P _ {D i}, Q _ {i} = Q _ {G i} - Q _ {D i}
\]
<|det|>text [19, 728, 87, 744]<|/det|>因而有
<|det|>equation [206, 753, 789, 924]<|/det|>\[
\left. \begin{array}{l} f _ {1} = P _ {G 1} - P _ {D 1} - U _ {1} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U _ {i} \left[ G _ {1 j} \cos \left(\delta_ {1} - \delta_ {j}\right) + B _ {1 j} \sin \left(\delta_ {1} - \delta_ {j}\right) \right] = 0 \\ \vdots \\ f _ {n} = P _ {G n} - P _ {D n} - U _ {n} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U _ {i} \left[ G _ {n j} \cos \left(\delta_ {n} - \delta_ {j}\right) + B _ {n j} \sin \left(\delta_ {n} - \delta_ {j}\right) \right] = 0 \\ f _ {n + 1} = Q _ {G 1} - Q _ {D 1} + U _ {1} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U _ {i} \left[ B _ {1 j} \cos \left(\delta_ {1} - \delta_ {j}\right) - G _ {1 j} \sin \left(\delta_ {1} - \delta_ {j}\right) \right] = 0 \\ \vdots \\ f _ {2 n + 1} = Q _ {G n} - Q _ {D n} - U _ {n} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} U _ {i} \left[ B _ {n j} \cos \left(\delta_ {n} - \delta_ {j}\right) - G _ {n j} \sin \left(\delta_ {n} - \delta_ {j}\right) \right] = 0 \end{array} \right\}
\]
