<|det|>title [231, 665, 330, 679]<|/det|>Conclusions
<|det|>text [73, 693, 487, 832]<|/det|>The matrix photochemistry of methyl thiofluorofornate,  \( \mathrm{FC(O)SCH_{3}} \) , follows closely the precedents set by the corresponding thiochloroformate,  \( \mathrm{ClC(O)SCH_{3}} \) . \( ^{[9]} \)  The various reaction channels have been charted by characterization of the photoproducts by their IR spectra and by monitoring the behavior of the IR absorptions as a function of photolysis time. The conclusions have been endorsed by studying the effects of perdeuteration of the parent compound and carrying out quantum chemical calculations on the putative products.
<|det|>text [74, 833, 487, 875]<|/det|>The photolytic interconversion of the two rotamers of  \( \mathrm{FC(O)SCH_{3}} \)  is the first process observed on broadband UV-visible irradiation of the matrix-isolated molecule, behavior
