<|det|>text [55, 223, 425, 274]<|/det|>(a) If \( f(x) = x\mathrm{e}^x \), find \( f'(x) \).
<|det|>text [55, 286, 545, 338]<|/det|>(b) Find the nth derivative,  \( f^{(n)}(x) \) .
<|det|>title [55, 416, 191, 460]<|/det|>Solution:
<|det|>text [55, 478, 552, 528]<|/det|>(a) By the Product Rule, we have
<|det|>equation [234, 553, 650, 932]<|/det|>\[
\begin{array}{l} f ^ {\prime} (x) = \frac {d}{d x} \left(x e ^ {x}\right) \\ = x \frac {d}{d x} \left(e ^ {x}\right) + e ^ {x} \frac {d}{d x} (x) \\ = x e ^ {x} + e ^ {x} \cdot 1 = (x + 1) e ^ {x} \\ \end{array}
\]
