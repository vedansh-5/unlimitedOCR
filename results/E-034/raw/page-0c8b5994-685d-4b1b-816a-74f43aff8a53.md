<|det|>text [148, 322, 695, 369]<|/det|>(1) 令 \( y = \sqrt{4 - (x - 3)^2} \Rightarrow y = \sqrt{4 - (x - 3)^2} \geq 0 \Rightarrow \begin{cases} y \geq 0 \\ (x - 3)^2 + y^2 = 4 \end{cases} \)
