7.3 Linear Systems of Equations. Gauss Elimination

At the end of the Gauss elimination (before the back substitution), the row echelon form of the augmented matrix will be

(9)

\[
\begin{array}{l} \left[ \begin{array}{c c c c c c} r _ {1 1} & r _ {1 2} & \dots & \dots & \dots & r _ {1 n} \\ & r _ {2 2} & \dots & \dots & \dots & r _ {2 n} \\ & & \ddots & \dots & \dots & \vdots \\ & & & r _ {r r} & \dots & r _ {r n} \\ \hline \end{array} \right] \left[ \begin{array}{c} f _ {1} \\ f _ {2} \\ \vdots \\ f _ {r} \\ f _ {r + 1} \\ \vdots \\ f _ {m} \end{array} \right] \\ \end{array}
\]

Here, and all entries in the blue triangle and blue rectangle are zero.
