\[
\lim _ {n \rightarrow \infty} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} f \left(0 + \frac {x - 0}{n} i\right) \frac {x - 0}{n} = \int_ {0} ^ {x} f (t) \mathrm{d} t.
\]

例5 设 \( f(x)=\left\{\begin{aligned}&\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n}\left(1+\cos\frac{x}{n}+\cos\frac{2x}{n}+\cdots+\cos\frac{n-1}{n}x\right),&x>0,\\&a,&x=0,\\&f(-x),&x<0\end{aligned}\right. \) 连续，则a= ____.

解 应填 1.

当 \(x > 0\) 时，\(f(x) = \lim_{n\to \infty}\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 0}^{n - 1}\cos \frac{x}{n} i = \lim_{n\to \infty}\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 0}^{n - 1}\cos \frac{x}{n} n\frac{x}{n}\)

\[
= \frac {1}{x} \int_ {0} ^ {x} \cos t \mathrm{d} t = \frac {1}{x} \sin x \Bigg | _ {0} ^ {x} = \frac {\sin x}{x};
\]

当 \(x < 0\) 时，\(f(x) = f(-x) = \frac{\sin(-x)}{-x} = \frac{\sin x}{x}\).

综上所述， \(f(x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\frac{\sin x}{x}, & x\neq 0,\\ a, & x = 0. \end{array} \right.\) 故由 \(f(x)\) 连续，得 \(a = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\sin x}{x} = 1\)
