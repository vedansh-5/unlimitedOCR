<|det|>text [56, 238, 978, 400]<|/det|>■ Solve for the basic variables  \( x_{1} \)  and  \( x_{2} \)  to obtain  \( x_{1}=\frac{4}{3}x_{3} \) ,  \( x_{2}=0 \) , with  \( x_{3} \)  free.
<|det|>text [56, 416, 975, 533]<|/det|>As a vector, the general solution of Ax = 0 has the form given below.
<|det|>equation [111, 543, 907, 920]<|/det|>\[
\mathbf {x} = \left[ \begin{array}{c} x _ {1} \\ x _ {2} \\ x _ {3} \end{array} \right] = \left[ \begin{array}{c} \frac {4}{3} x _ {3} \\ 0 \\ x _ {3} \end{array} \right] = x _ {3} \left[ \begin{array}{c} \frac {4}{3} \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{array} \right] = x _ {3} \mathbf {v}, \text {where} \mathbf {v} = \left[ \begin{array}{c} \frac {4}{3} \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{array} \right]
\]
