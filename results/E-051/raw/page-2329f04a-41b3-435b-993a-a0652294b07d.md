<|det|>text [201, 230, 797, 288]<|/det|>The following theorem bridges the spectral analysis on \( X_{\Gamma} = \Gamma \backslash G / H \) and recent progress of branching problems that we outlined in Sections 2 to 4.
<|det|>text [202, 307, 798, 412]<|/det|>Theorem 5.13. Let \( X = G / H \) be a reductive symmetric pair. Suppose that a reductive subgroup \( G' \) of \( G \) acts properly on \( X \) and that the complexification \( G_{\mathbb{C}}' \) acts spherically on \( X_{\mathbb{C}} \). Then the restriction \( \Pi|_{G'} \) is \( G' \)-admissible (Definition 2.1) for any \( H \)-distinguished \( \Pi \in \widehat{G} \) (Definition 4.2). Moreover, the multiplicities are uniformly bounded:
<|det|>equation [368, 426, 630, 456]<|/det|>\[
\sup _ {\Pi \in \operatorname{Irr} (G) _ {H}} \sup _ {\pi \in \operatorname{Irr} (G ^ {\prime})} [ \Pi | _ {G ^ {\prime}}: \pi ] <   \infty .
\]
<|det|>title [202, 468, 611, 488]<|/det|>5.7. \(H \subset G \supset G' \rightsquigarrow H \xrightarrow{\text{ind}_X} G \searrow^{\text{rest}} G'\).
<|det|>text [201, 491, 795, 551]<|/det|>The conclusion of Theorem 5.13 follows from two key aspects of branching problems: \( G' \)-admissibility, as discussed in Section 2 and the uniformly bounded multiplicities outlined in Sections 3 and 4.
