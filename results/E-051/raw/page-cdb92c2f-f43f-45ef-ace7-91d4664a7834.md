<|det|>text [124, 118, 875, 173]<|/det|>Let us consider that \((\mathcal{L},[\cdot_{\lambda}\cdot],\alpha)\) is a Hom-Leibniz conformal algebra, which has coefficients in the representation \((l,r,\mathcal{M},\beta)\). The group of \(n\)th-cochain \(C_{HomL}^{n}(\mathcal{L},M)\) contains the map \(f_{\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_{n-1}}:\wedge^{\otimes n}\mathcal{L}\to \mathbb{C}[\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_{n-1}]\otimes \mathcal{M}\) defined by \(p_1\otimes p_2\otimes \dots \otimes p_n\mapsto f_{\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\dots,\lambda_{n-1}}(p_1,p_2,\dots,p_n)\). These maps satisfy the following conformal sesquilinearity condition:
<|det|>equation [196, 176, 871, 233]<|/det|>\[
\begin{array}{l} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} (p _ {1}, p _ {2}, \dots , \partial (p _ {i}), \dots , p _ {n}) \\ = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} - \lambda_ {i} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} (p _ {1}, p ^ {\prime}, \dots , p _ {n}), & i = 1, \dots , n - 1, \\ (\partial + \lambda_ {1} + \lambda_ {2} + \dots + \lambda_ {n - 1}) f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} (p _ {1}, p _ {2}, \dots , p _ {n}), & i = n. \end{array} \right. \tag {12} \\ \end{array}
\]
<|det|>text [124, 235, 820, 251]<|/det|>such that \( f \circ \alpha^{\otimes n} = \beta \circ f \). The coboundary map \( \delta_{Hom}: C_{Hom}^{n}(\mathcal{L}, \mathcal{M}) \to C_{Hom}^{n+1}(\mathcal{L}, \mathcal{M}) \) can be defined by
<|det|>equation [196, 254, 871, 359]<|/det|>\[
\begin{array}{l} (\delta_ {H o m L} f) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} (p _ {1}, \dots , p _ {n + 1}) \\ = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} (- 1) ^ {i + 1} l \left(\alpha^ {n - 1} \left(p _ {i}\right)\right) _ {\lambda_ {i}} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda} _ {i}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} \left(p _ {1}, \dots , \hat {p} _ {i}, \dots , p _ {n + 1}\right) \tag {13} \\ + (- 1) ^ {n + 1} r (f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} (p _ {1}, \dots , p _ {n})) _ {\lambda_ {1} + \dots + \lambda_ {n}} \alpha^ {n - 1} (p _ {n + 1}) \\ + \sum_ {i <   j} (- 1) ^ {i + j} f _ {\lambda_ {i} + \lambda_ {j}, \lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {j}}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} ([ p _ {i \lambda_ {i}} p _ {j} ], \alpha (p _ {1}), \dots , \alpha (\hat {p} _ {j}), \dots , \alpha (\hat {p} _ {n}), \dots , \alpha (p _ {n + 1})), \\ \end{array}
\]
<|det|>text [124, 363, 873, 405]<|/det|>where \( f \in C_{HomL}^n(\mathcal{L}, \mathcal{M}) \) and \( p_1, p_2, \dots, p_n, p_{n+1} \in \mathcal{L} \). With coefficients in the representation \( (l, r, \mathcal{M}, \beta) \), the corresponding cohomology groups are known as the cohomology of the Hom-Leibniz conformal algebra \( (\mathcal{L}, [\cdot, \cdot], \alpha) \). If we assume that \( l(p)_{\lambda}m = [p_{\lambda}m] \) and \( r(m)_{\lambda}p = [m_{\lambda}p] \) then above equations turns to be
<|det|>equation [194, 407, 872, 515]<|/det|>\[
\begin{array}{l} (\delta_ {H o m L} f) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} (p _ {1}, \dots , p _ {n + 1}) \\ = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} (- 1) ^ {i + 1} \left[ \alpha^ {n - 1} \left(p _ {i}\right) _ {\lambda_ {i}} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda} _ {i}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} \left(p _ {1}, \dots , \hat {p} _ {i}, \dots , p _ {n + 1}\right) \right] \tag {14} \\ + (- 1) ^ {n + 1} \left[ f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} \left(p _ {1}, \dots , p _ {n}\right) _ {\lambda_ {1} + \dots + \lambda_ {n}} \alpha^ {n - 1} \left(p _ {n + 1}\right) \right] \\ + \sum_ {i <   j} (- 1) ^ {i + j} f _ {\lambda_ {i} + \lambda_ {j}, \lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \hat {\lambda_ {j}}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} ([ p _ {i \lambda_ {1}} p _ {j} ], \alpha (p _ {1}), \dots , \alpha (\hat {p} _ {i}), \dots , \alpha (p _ {n + 1})), \\ \end{array}
\]
